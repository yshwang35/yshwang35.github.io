Youngseok Hwang

I'm an integrated Ph.D. student at the Graduate School of Data Science, Seoul National University, where I'm a member of the Data Intelligence and Learning (DIAL) Lab advised by Hyunwoo Park.

I'm fortunate to collaborate with Wonhee Lee at KRISO on maritime AI (AI for Science) and with Jaeyeon Kim at A*STAR on 3D CAD reconstruction (AI for Engineering). In the lab, I also work on heterogeneous graph learning.

Research

I'm interested in AI for Science and AI for Engineering, both built on representation learning for graph and tabular data. My current focus:

  • AI for Science: benchmarks and models for physical systems with scarce labels but rich domain knowledge, such as vessel traffic and industrial sensor networks.
  • AI for Engineering: reconstructing editable CAD programs from text, multi-view images, and point clouds.
  • Heterogeneous Graph Learning: understanding when graph structure actually helps on heterogeneous text-attributed graphs.
