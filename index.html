I'm an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student at the Graduate School of Data Science, Seoul National University, where I'm a member of the DIAL Lab advised by Hyunwoo Park. I'm also advised by Joonseok Lee.

I work with KRISO on maritime AI. Before grad school, I studied mechanical engineering.

Research

I'm interested in AI for science, graph representation learning, and tabular data learning. Much of my work is on maritime anomaly detection with spatio-temporal graphs, heterogeneous text-attributed graphs, and 3D CAD reconstruction. In the long run, I want to contribute to physical AI.
