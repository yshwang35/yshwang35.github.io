Research

I'm interested in AI for science and AI for engineering. For science, I build benchmarks and models for physical systems such as vessel traffic and industrial sensor networks, where anomalies are rare and labels are scarce, but domain knowledge like kinematic equations and system design is rich. For engineering, I work on reconstructing editable CAD programs from text, images, and point clouds. Underlying both, I'm interested in graph and tabular representation learning, and in understanding when graph structure actually helps.
